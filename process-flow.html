Key features that protect us:
  • FIFO automation — packers can't accidentally pick the wrong container; the system picks the oldest active one for them.
  • Bucket traceability — every 5kg bar bucket is uniquely identified, tracked through every dispatch, and audited on return.
  • Shop QR confirmation — drivers / shop managers confirm receipt with a single scan on a dedicated public page. No paperwork.
  • Wholesale, online & staff — auto-confirmed at pack time. Same flow, no QR overhead.
  • Staff takeaway tracking — coffee given to staff is logged + deducted from container stock. Rules out staff as a cause when stock looks short.
  • Test roasts — separate from production metrics. No container assigned, 1 batch max, green stock still deducted.
  • Missing-items reporting — shops can flag a delivery as incomplete during confirmation. Flagged dispatches surface in the dispatch report.
  • Empty container weights — optional per-container empty kg means roasters can weigh gross (no zeroing) and the system calculates net.
  • Full audit trail — from green delivery (with invoice) to shop confirmation. Every kg accounted for.
  • Roaster attribution — every roast is tagged to the logged-in roaster. Reports show consistency per roaster.
  • Bucket condition tracking — clean / dirty / damaged / lost on return. Reports flag shops mistreating buckets.
